# Live Demo Video Script

Target length: 4–5 minutes.

1. Open https://omyaingle.vercel.app on desktop and show the wallet onboarding
   choices.
2. Connect a Freighter testnet wallet and show the four-step onboarding card.
3. Fund the wallet or show its testnet balance.
4. Open AI Chat and run a balance or portfolio command.
5. Open Spending Limits and demonstrate the wallet guard controls.
6. Open **Activity Proof**, refresh the real ledger records, open one Stellar
   Expert transaction, and export the evidence CSV.
7. Explain the 50-interaction target and the 120–180 second interval validator.
8. Open Analytics and show portfolio, activity, and health monitoring.
9. Open Feedback and submit a consented test response.
10. Resize to a mobile viewport and demonstrate the bottom navigation.
11. Show both testnet contract addresses on Stellar Expert.
12. Show the pitch deck, feedback workbook, public GitHub README, commit count,
    and passing CI run.

Do not record the final evidence segment until genuine participant transactions
are available. Upload the completed recording to an accessible YouTube, Loom, or
Google Drive URL and add the link to the README and `SUBMISSION.md`.
